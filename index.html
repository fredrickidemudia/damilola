Elani The Elephant

Living in a dense, sun-dappled forest, Elani felt isolated because of her massive size. When she asked a monkey, a rabbit, and a frog to play, they all refused, claiming she was simply too big to join in their games. Heartbroken, Elani wandered off to a quiet corner of the jungle.

The very next day, a sudden panic swept through the trees. A fierce tiger began chasing the smaller animals, cornering them near a rocky cliff. Realizing they were trapped, Elani did not hesitate. She charged forward, raised her trunk, and let out a deafening trumpet before giving the tiger a mighty kick that sent it running away in terror.

The astonished animals realized that Elani's immense size was exactly what made her a hero. From that day on, she was never lonely again, proving that true friends come in all shapes and sizes.
